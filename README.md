Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI
Project Overview
This project presents an interactive Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI.
The dashboard provides insights into Adidas sales performance, profitability, regional distribution, retailer performance, and product-wise sales.
It helps transform sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations, KPI cards, maps, and filters.

Project Objectives
Analyze overall Adidas sales performance.
Monitor total sales and profitability.
Identify monthly sales trends.
Compare sales across different US states and regions.
Evaluate retailer-wise sales performance.
Analyze product-wise sales contributions.
Understand sales performance across different sales channels.
Compare business performance across different years.

Dashboard Features
1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
Total Sales
Total Profit
Total Units Sold
Average Price
Average Profit Margin
2. Sales Analysis
Monthly Sales Trend
State-wise Sales Analysis
Region-wise Sales Distribution
3. Retailer Analysis
Retailer-wise Sales Performance
Comparison of sales across major retailers
4. Product Analysis
Product-wise Sales Performance
Comparison of sales across different Adidas product categories
5. Interactive Filters
Year: 2020 and 2021
Sales Channel: In-store, Online, Outlet
Month: January to December
